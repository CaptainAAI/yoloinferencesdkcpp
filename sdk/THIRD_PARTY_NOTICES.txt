YoloHttp SDK - third-party components
=====================================

YoloHttp.dll compiles in RapidJSON (header-only). The DLLs in bin\ other than
YoloHttp.dll are shipped as is. Each component keeps its own license; the
license texts are in this folder.

Component                       License                    File
------------------------------  -------------------------  ------------------------------
libcurl (libcurl.dll)           curl License (MIT style)   curl-COPYING.txt
zlib (z.dll, used by libcurl)   zlib License               zlib-LICENSE.txt
RapidJSON                       MIT                        rapidjson-LICENSE.txt
OpenCV 5.0 (opencv_world500)    Apache License 2.0         OpenCV-LICENSE.txt

Upstream projects:
  https://curl.se/
  https://github.com/madler/zlib
  https://github.com/Tencent/rapidjson
